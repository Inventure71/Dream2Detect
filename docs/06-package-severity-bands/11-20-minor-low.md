# Band 11-20: Minor Low

## Role In The Scale

This is the entry band for clearly visible but light package damage.

It is the first band where a reviewer should be able to say:

> yes, there is a real visible defect

But the package should still look mostly intact overall.

## Core Definition

This band should contain packages with one small, light, unambiguous defect and no serious structural compromise.

## Expected Defect Profile

Typical examples:

- one small dent,
- one lightly softened corner,
- one short superficial tear,
- one light crease from handling,
- one noticeable but limited stain or scuff that clearly worsens appearance.

The key idea is:

- damage is visible,
- damage is real,
- damage is still clearly light.

## Required Visual Characteristics

- overall box shape remains normal,
- most faces remain clean and geometrically stable,
- damage is localized and limited,
- no clear crushing,
- no large tears,
- no hole,
- no opening,
- no major flap damage.

## What Counts As Too Weak

If the defect is so small that a reasonable person would still call the package visually intact, it should go down to `0-10`.

## What Counts As Too Strong

Move the image upward if:

- more than one meaningful damage area is visible,
- the dent clearly changes the face geometry,
- a corner is obviously damaged rather than just slightly softened,
- the tear is not tiny anymore,
- the defect begins to change the overall impression from "lightly affected" to "clearly degraded."

## Dirt And Stain Rule

Dirt or stain may place an image here only if:

- it is noticeable,
- it clearly worsens visible condition,
- it remains limited and light.

Tiny dust or ordinary shipping wear should not drive this band.

## Visual Impression Test

A normal viewer should say something like:

- slightly damaged,
- lightly dented,
- a little worn,
- minor visible defect.

They should **not** say:

- crushed,
- badly torn,
- heavily damaged,
- structurally compromised.

## Prompt Guidance

Prompt for:

- package mostly intact,
- one small visible defect,
- light dent, slight corner softening, tiny tear, or limited cosmetic damage,
- no major deformation,
- no hole,
- no major tear,
- no heavy crushing.

Preferred prompt words:

- slight
- light
- small
- limited
- mostly intact
- one minor visible defect

Do not prompt:

- multiple major defects,
- collapsed corners,
- broken geometry,
- large openings,
- severe stain spread,
- exposed interior,
- torn open corner,
- strong crushing,
- deep face collapse.

## Labeling Guidance

Assign `11-20` when:

- the defect is unmistakably real,
- the package still looks mostly intact,
- the condition is only lightly worse than normal.
