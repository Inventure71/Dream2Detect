# Band 46-55: Moderate Mid

## Role In The Scale

This is the center of the `moderate` class.

It should represent the most typical form of clear, meaningful package damage without reaching the strongest `severe` category.

## Core Definition

This band should contain packages whose damage is substantial, obvious, and hard to downplay, but not yet clearly catastrophic.

## Expected Defect Profile

Typical examples:

- a clearly dented or deformed face,
- a strongly damaged corner or edge,
- a clearly visible tear that meaningfully affects condition,
- a small but real hole or opening,
- multiple visible defects combining into one strong moderate impression,
- partial crushing that is clear but not extreme.

## Required Visual Characteristics

- the package looks meaningfully damaged at first glance,
- geometry is visibly less clean and less intact,
- at least one defect should be central and obvious,
- several smaller defects may combine into a strong total impression,
- the package remains recognizable and not fully destroyed.

## Boundary Logic

Compared with `36-45`, this band should feel:

- more obvious,
- more central,
- harder to dismiss,
- less borderline.

Compared with `56-65`, this band should still stop short of:

- near-severe collapse,
- very large openings,
- very strong structural failure,
- damage that dominates almost the entire package.

## Dirt And Stain Rule

Heavy cosmetic worsening may contribute here, but most images in this band should still include meaningful structural or material defect signals.

## Visual Impression Test

A normal viewer should say:

- this box is clearly in bad condition,
- definitely moderate damage,
- visibly compromised,
- not yet the worst category.

## Prompt Guidance

Prompt for:

- obvious moderate package damage,
- strong denting, visible tear, damaged corner, partial crushing, or combined defects,
- clearly degraded geometry,
- still not fully collapsed or catastrophic.

Calibration note:

- this band can include a small opening,
- but the opening should not dominate the image the way a severe corner tear would.

Avoid:

- slight wear only,
- near-intact package structure,
- package-wide collapse that clearly belongs in `severe`,
- torn-open cavity that dominates the corner,
- severe structural breakage across multiple major areas.

## Labeling Guidance

Assign `46-55` when:

- `moderate` is the clear class,
- the damage feels central and obvious,
- but the package is still short of severe failure.
