# Band 66-75: Severe Low

## Role In The Scale

This is the entry band for `severe`.

It marks the point where the package should now be described as badly damaged rather than merely strongly moderate.

## Core Definition

This band should contain clearly severe package damage: strong visible structural or material failure that immediately communicates serious condition loss.

## Expected Defect Profile

Typical examples:

- strong crushing in a central area,
- one major damaged corner or edge with clear structural failure,
- one large tear or strong opening,
- geometry that is clearly broken rather than only degraded,
- multiple strong defects combining into a severe overall impression.

## Required Visual Characteristics

- serious visible damage dominates the package impression,
- the package no longer looks merely degraded,
- at least one major defect is obvious,
- geometry may be broken, collapsed, or strongly deformed,
- material failure may be central.

## What Keeps It At The Low End Of Severe

This band should still avoid the most extreme forms of collapse or destruction.

It is severe, but not maximal.

## Boundary With Moderate

Move the image down only if:

- the package is still very damaged but not obviously severe,
- geometry is degraded rather than broken,
- openings or tears are serious but not yet major.

If a viewer immediately describes the box as badly damaged, severe should usually win.

## Visual Impression Test

A normal viewer should say:

- badly damaged,
- clearly severe,
- strongly crushed or torn,
- not the absolute worst, but clearly beyond moderate.

## Prompt Guidance

Prompt for:

- clearly severe package damage,
- strong crushing, major corner failure, strong tear/opening, or clearly broken geometry,
- serious visible condition loss,
- still short of total collapse or extreme destruction.

Preferred severe-entry phrasing:

- torn-open corner
- major opening
- clear structural failure
- broken geometry
- badly damaged

Avoid:

- damage that still reads as merely moderate,
- tiny openings,
- mostly preserved geometry,
- subtle or low-force damage wording.

## Labeling Guidance

Assign `66-75` when:

- `severe` is clearly the right class,
- but the package is not yet near the top end of the entire severity scale.
