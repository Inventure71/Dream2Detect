# Band 36-45: Moderate Low

## Role In The Scale

This is the entry band for `moderate`.

It marks the point where the package should no longer be described as only lightly damaged.

## Core Definition

This band should contain packages with substantial visible damage that clearly degrades visible condition, while still staying near the lower edge of `moderate`.

## Expected Defect Profile

Typical examples:

- one clearly damaged corner or edge,
- one noticeable face deformation,
- one visible tear that is more than superficial,
- several smaller defects that together create a substantial damage impression,
- early partial crushing,
- one small opening or very small hole if clearly visible but limited.

## Required Visual Characteristics

- damage is unambiguous and meaningful,
- package condition is clearly degraded,
- geometry may begin to look less clean and less stable,
- one defect may dominate or several smaller ones may combine,
- the image should no longer read as "mostly fine."

## What Keeps It At The Low End Of Moderate

This band should still avoid:

- major collapse,
- broad severe crushing,
- large openings,
- catastrophic material failure,
- package-wide destruction.

## Boundary With Minor

Move the image down only if:

- the damage still feels local and light,
- overall condition still feels mostly intact,
- geometry loss is weak.

If a reasonable reviewer immediately sees the package as clearly degraded, it belongs in `moderate` or higher.

## Visual Impression Test

A normal viewer should say:

- clearly damaged,
- noticeably deformed or torn,
- more than a small defect,
- not yet badly destroyed.

## Prompt Guidance

Prompt for:

- clearly damaged package,
- one meaningful damaged corner, dented face, visible limited tear, or several smaller defects,
- some loss of clean box appearance,
- still short of major collapse or heavy severe failure.

Preferred phrasing for this band:

- frayed corner
- partially torn edge
- dented face
- clearly damaged but not near-severe
- limited tear without major opening

This band should usually avoid visible access into the box interior.

Avoid:

- barely visible damage,
- essentially intact geometry,
- extreme destruction that belongs in `severe`,
- torn-open corner,
- large visible opening,
- exposed interior cavity,
- strong material failure dominating the image.

## Labeling Guidance

Assign `36-45` when:

- the package has crossed out of minor damage,
- the condition is substantially worse than normal,
- but the image still sits near the lower boundary of `moderate`.
