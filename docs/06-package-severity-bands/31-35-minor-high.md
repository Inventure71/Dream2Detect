# Band 31-35: Minor High

## Role In The Scale

This is the top end of the `minor` class.

It should contain packages that are close to `moderate`, but not quite there yet.

## Core Definition

This band should contain strong minor damage: clearly visible, noticeable, and non-trivial, but still not substantial enough to justify the `moderate` label.

## Expected Defect Profile

Typical examples:

- one prominent dent that is still limited in scope,
- one clearly damaged corner without major collapse,
- one visible tear that remains limited,
- a few small defects that together create a clear minor-damage impression,
- one stronger cosmetic degradation pattern without broad structural failure.

## Required Visual Characteristics

- the package is visibly damaged without argument,
- overall geometry is still largely preserved,
- no small hole yet unless it is extremely borderline and not the dominant defect,
- no substantial crushing,
- no large opening,
- no clearly degraded box structure across multiple major areas.

## Boundary With Moderate

Keep the image in `31-35` only if:

- damage is still more local than substantial,
- the package still reads as "mostly intact but visibly damaged,"
- the defect has not yet crossed into clear loss of box integrity.

Move it into `36-45` if:

- the box shape is clearly degraded,
- multiple areas interact to create a substantial damage impression,
- a tear or opening becomes clearly more than minor,
- a corner or edge failure becomes a central visible feature.

## Dirt And Stain Rule

Dirt or stain may contribute here if it is clearly visible and non-trivial, but this band should still not feel like broad heavy degradation from contamination alone.

## Visual Impression Test

A normal viewer should say:

- fairly damaged, but not badly damaged,
- close to moderate,
- more than light wear,
- still short of substantial compromise.

## Prompt Guidance

Prompt for:

- noticeably damaged package,
- damage stronger than light minor wear,
- limited but prominent denting, corner damage, or tearing,
- geometry still mostly preserved,
- no heavy crushing,
- no obvious major opening.

Calibration note:

- this band may look close to moderate,
- but it must still avoid damage that exposes the interior or creates a strong structural failure impression.

Avoid:

- multiple strongly damaged faces,
- collapse,
- broad severe deformation,
- clear moderate-or-worse structure loss,
- torn-open edge,
- large corner opening,
- obvious cavity into the box interior.

## Labeling Guidance

Assign `31-35` when:

- `minor` still feels correct,
- but the package is near the upper boundary of that class,
- and the next step up to `moderate` would require only a modest increase in visible severity.
