# Band 86-100: Severe High

## Role In The Scale

This is the top end of the entire severity scale.

It should be reserved for the most extreme visible package damage in the dataset.

## Core Definition

This band should contain packages with very strong structural collapse, major material failure, or extreme visible condition loss.

## Expected Defect Profile

Typical examples:

- very strong crushing or collapse,
- large destroyed or opened sections,
- major tears with obvious material failure,
- severe multi-face deformation,
- heavily broken geometry across large areas,
- damage so strong that it dominates nearly the entire package impression.

## Required Visual Characteristics

- severe damage is overwhelming and central,
- the package looks heavily compromised,
- multiple parts may be visibly broken, collapsed, or opened,
- overall geometry is far from normal,
- the package must still be recognizable as a package or box.

## Reservation Rule

Do not overuse this band.

This band is for the worst visible damage examples, not just any severe image.

If the package is clearly severe but not near the top of the scale, it should likely stay in `66-75` or `76-85`.

## Visual Impression Test

A normal viewer should say:

- extremely damaged,
- heavily crushed,
- badly broken,
- near the worst condition expected in this project.

## Prompt Guidance

Prompt for:

- extreme but still recognizable package damage,
- major collapse, very large tears/openings, heavy crushing, strong multi-area failure,
- maximum visible severity within the dataset scale.

Preferred phrasing:

- extreme visible damage
- major collapse
- very large opening
- heavily broken geometry
- strongest severity in the dataset

Avoid:

- moderate-severe but not extreme cases,
- images where the object stops looking like a package entirely,
- chaotic scenes where the package is unreadable and cannot be consistently labeled.

## Labeling Guidance

Assign `86-100` when:

- the package clearly belongs at the top end of the scale,
- the visible damage is among the strongest examples in the dataset,
- and a lower severe band would understate the condition.
