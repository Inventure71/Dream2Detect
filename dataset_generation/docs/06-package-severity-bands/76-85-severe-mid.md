# Band 76-85: Severe Mid

## Role In The Scale

This is the center of the `severe` class.

It should represent strongly severe damage that is obvious, dominant, and hard to misclassify.

## Core Definition

This band should contain packages with major visible structural breakdown or heavy material damage that dominates the image.

## Expected Defect Profile

Typical examples:

- large-scale crushing,
- major face collapse,
- large tear or opening,
- strong multi-area deformation,
- badly compromised corners and edges,
- several major defects interacting at once.

## Required Visual Characteristics

- the package looks badly damaged immediately,
- the geometry is clearly compromised,
- material failure or crushing is not subtle,
- damage dominates more than one local cue,
- the package is still recognizable as a package.

## Boundary Logic

Compared with `66-75`, this band should feel:

- more dominant,
- broader in effect,
- harder to call merely entry-level severe.

Compared with `86-100`, it should still stop short of the most extreme destruction or maximal breakdown.

## Dirt And Stain Rule

Severe dirt or staining may contribute, but this band should almost always involve strong structural or material damage, not cosmetic degradation alone.

## Visual Impression Test

A normal viewer should say:

- heavily damaged,
- badly crushed,
- seriously torn,
- clearly in very poor condition.

## Prompt Guidance

Prompt for:

- heavy visible package damage,
- large crushing, strong broken geometry, major tears/openings, badly damaged corners/edges,
- severe overall condition,
- still recognizable as a box.

Calibration note:

- severe must dominate immediately,
- but the package should still remain visually coherent enough to label reliably.

Avoid:

- milder severe cases that belong in `66-75`,
- total destruction that no longer resembles a usable box at all unless that is still visually within recognizability.

## Labeling Guidance

Assign `76-85` when:

- the package is plainly heavily damaged,
- the severe label is unquestionable,
- and the image sits around the center of the severe range rather than at its lower or upper edge.
